A Living Record of Achievement

Not fame for fame's sake.
Impact with substance.

This page separates well-known Sorors from honorary members and uses Member Spotlight to feature women whose impact deserves deeper attention. Chapter information appears only when it is known and documented; it is not used as a separate public-facing category.
