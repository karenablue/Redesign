Service & Impact

Foundations That Expand the Work

Through education, research, advocacy, fiscal support, and strategic partnerships, Sigma Gamma Rho's partner foundations help transform community vision into sustained impact.

National Education Fund SPEAR Foundation
FOUNDATIONS

Extending Greater Service

Purpose Backed by Infrastructure

Community service grows stronger when it is supported by institutions designed to fund opportunity, develop leaders, strengthen programs, and turn ideas into sustainable action.

Two partner foundations play distinct but complementary roles in that work: the National Education Fund and the Sigma Public Education and Research Foundation.

National Education Fund

Education • Opportunity • Leadership

National Education Fund

Expanding educational opportunity by helping students overcome financial barriers and preparing future leaders to thrive.

The Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority National Education Fund, Inc. is the charitable foundation of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. Its work centers scholarships, leadership development, and community education designed to improve academic opportunity and quality of life.

Mission

The National Education Fund provides scholarship assistance to students with financial need while supporting educational programs, workshops, symposiums, and forums that promote learning, leadership, and stronger communities.

SPEAR Foundation

Research • Advocacy • Community Support

SPEAR Foundation

Helping communities turn vision into action through strategic support, public education, research, and sustainable partnerships.

The Sigma Public Education and Research Foundation advances social, civic, cultural, economic, and educational progress by empowering vulnerable and at-risk families through service, education, advocacy, and research.

Through fiscal sponsorship and strategic partnerships, SPEAR supports Sigma Gamma Rho chapters and community-based organizations as they develop programs designed to reduce poverty and strengthen communities.

Visit the SPEAR Foundation ↗
01

Advocacy & Research

Supporting evidence-based advocacy and research that informs solutions and expands opportunity.

02

Fiscal Sponsorship

Equipping chapters and community organizations with resources to develop and sustain meaningful programs.

03

Community Impact

Supporting public education and community-service initiatives that address the needs of vulnerable families.

Shared Purpose

Different Roles. One Greater Mission.

Scholarships create access. Leadership development builds capacity. Research strengthens decisions. Fiscal sponsorship helps programs grow. Together, these foundations extend the infrastructure behind Greater Service.
