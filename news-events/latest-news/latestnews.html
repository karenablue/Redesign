Tampa Welcomes Sigma Gamma Rho for the 61st International Biennial Boule

Thousands of members, partners, and guests gathered at the Tampa Convention Center July 22–26 for the 61st International Biennial Boule — the first Sigma Gamma Rho international convention held in Tampa since 1976. The city projected the gathering would generate more than $5.1 million in direct regional economic impact, alongside on-the-ground service including 300 newborn care packages, 3,000 backpacks for K–8 students, and 300 free swim lessons for local youth. “Our presence in Tampa represents an investment in people, partnerships, and progress,” said 26th International Grand Basileus Marica T. Harris.
