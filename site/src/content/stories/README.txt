Chapter stories

One .md file per story. Rules and fields are in src/content.config.ts ("stories").
Publish only what a chapter submitted. A photo needs imageAlt and
photoConsent: true (everyone pictured agreed to publication).

Example, saved as e.g. 2026-10-18-example-chapter-book-drive.md:

---
title: "Example Chapter fills 400 backpacks for Operation BigBookBag"
chapter: Example Sigma Chapter
region: Southeastern
place: "Atlanta, GA"
date: 2026-10-18
program: Operation BigBookBag
image: /images/stories/2026-10-18-example.jpg
imageAlt: "Sorors packing backpacks with school supplies in a church hall"
photoConsent: true
---

The story itself, about 150 words, in the chapter's own words, lightly edited.
