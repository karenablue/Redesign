Le’Daviah Terry is an outgoing and dedicated leader whose approach to service and leadership is intentional and people-oriented. Though she has been a member of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. for a year, Le’Daviah has already demonstrated a rhoyal standard of excellence, leading with dignity, authenticity and respect in every space she enters.

She currently serves as Vice President of the Winthrop University Ambassador Program where she represents the university with integrity while remaining responsive to the evolving needs of students, stakeholders and the surrounding community. Her leadership is grounded in active service through donating supplies, organizing events, participating in St. Jude Fundraiser Walks and supporting Adopt-A-Highway cleanups. She also serves as Historian of Winthrop’s NPHC and Step Master for her chapter, Lambda Kappa.

Within the Winthrop community, Le’Daviah also serves as a Winthrop LIFE Peer Mentor where she remains consistently ready to serve by supporting students’ personal and academic growth.

Academically, Le’Daviah is majoring in Psychology with a minor in Music and Educational Studies. She aspires to become a Speech-Language Pathologist using music as a therapeutic tool to support children’s development. Professionally, she has participated in leadership programs such as the Best of the East Undergraduate Summit and the Southeastern Leadership Conference and was recently crowned First Runner Up to the 2025 Miss Black Talented Teen USA.
