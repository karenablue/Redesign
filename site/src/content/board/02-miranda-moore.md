International First Grand Anti-Basileus Miranda Moore has consistently served as an active and financial member of Sigma Gamma Rho for 32 years. Her Sigma journey began in Fall 1991 as an undergraduate in the exquisite Eta Alpha Chapter at the University of North Texas as the re-chartering Basileus (President). Serving as a collegiate member of the International Board of Directors as Southwestern Region Youth Services Coordinator, she continued her dedication to service and leadership over the years in various local, regional, and international positions, including, but not limited to, Southwestern Region Centennial Syntaktes (Regional Director), Southwestern Region Undergraduate Chapter Coordinator, and International Special Projects Chair during which tenure she was awarded Sigma of the Year.

First Grand Moore is a proud Ruby member, grateful for her legacy lineage in the sisterhood, and happy to be Life Member #893. Her dedication to service over the years have yielded partnerships with organizations like National Football League (NFL), Department of Transportation, National Highway Safety and Traffic Administration, National Conference of Black Mayors, City of Houston, Six Flags Texas/Hurricane Harbor, and many more. Her legacy is one of unwavering dedication and leadership excellence.

Professionally, First Grand Moore possesses a wealth of experience in Human Capital Management (HCM) and Workforce Management spanning over two decades. With over ten years of human resources experience as a recruiter, consultant, and executive, Ms. Moore has honed a diverse skill set, showcasing proficiency in client engagement, project management, diversity and inclusion, training, and change management. Her strategic and analytical approach, coupled with strong communication skills, underscores her effectiveness in implementing new policies and procedures.

In her current role as a Consultant and Enterprise Success Partner with an international software company, Ms. Moore manages client accounts within the Fortune 1000 in various industries. Her level of consulting expands across all arenas of business including Human Resources, Finance, Operations, Technology, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Procurement, CRM, Customer Experience, and People Management.

Beyond her sorority and corporate endeavors, First Grand Moore is a business owner, board member, and community leader. On a personal note, she enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling, and time at the spa. She looks forward to using her broad range of experience in the role of First Grand Anti Basileus.
