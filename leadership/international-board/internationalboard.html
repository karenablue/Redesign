International Board
of Directors

Our International Board of Directors provides strategic leadership to uphold the legacy of our seven illustrious founders and the thousands of celebrated women before us as we seek to further instill Sigma Gamma Rho’s core values across the globe.

Board Composition

One Collaborative
Governing Body.

Chaired by the International Grand Basileus, our collaborative Board consists of the National Officers, Founders, and the Regional Syntaktes, Undergraduate Chapter Coordinator, and Youth Services Coordinator of each region.

Past Grand Basilei serve on the board in an honorary capacity, connecting the Board’s present-day decisions to the Sorority’s full history of leadership.
