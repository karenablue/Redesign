Leadership Through the Decades

The Office
Across Time.

The women below represent the succession of Grand Basilei listed by Sigma Gamma Rho from the founding era through 2024. Their administrations span the Sorority's earliest organizational years, regional growth, national expansion, and entry into its second century.

Dr. Lorraine A. Williams served two nonconsecutive terms, and Mary Lou Allison Gardner Little is identified as the first elected Grand Basileus with an additional acting period in 1925–1926.
