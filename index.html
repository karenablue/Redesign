Who are We?
A Legacy of Leadership & Service
On November 12, 1922, seven young Black educators founded Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Incorporated at Butler University in Indianapolis, Indiana.
Establishing a sorority for Black women on a predominantly white campus during the height of racial segregation was a profound act of courage and vision. Our founders forged a sanctuary of intellectual rigor and mutual support, determined to advance educational opportunities and civil rights. That foundational commitment to academic excellence and community uplift remains the bedrock of our sisterhood today.
"We don't just join an organization. We carry forward a legacy of leadership, service, and progress."
MARICA T. HARRIS
International Grand Basileus
Today, Sigma Gamma Rho® advances that legacy through rigorous leadership development, targeted community investment, and global civic engagement — a shared responsibility to lead and create concrete change.

Join Our Sisterhood
Becoming a Member of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Incorporated® Is a Commitment
A commitment to scholarship, uncompromising sisterhood, and the profound responsibility to advance our communities. We seek women who understand that our legacy—forged by seven Black educators in 1922—demands action, integrity, and lifelong dedication.
We invite candidates who are prepared to invest their time, intellectual rigor, and leadership into tangible public service.
We are not seeking individuals who compromise academic excellence or personal accountability.
Membership in Sigma Gamma Rho is an active pursuit of our founding mandate: Greater Service, Greater Progress.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Thinking About Joining?
The membership intake process, known as the T.O.R.C.H. process, is a rigorous educational experience designed to ensure prospective members comprehend the profound history, governing policies, and high expectations of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. It demands leadership development, intellectual discipline, and a proven readiness for community service.
No formal invitation is required. Candidates are evaluated through official chapter and campus intake communications—not personal invitations, private promises, or social media. Use the Chapter Locator to connect with an authorized chapter near you.
Membership carries financial responsibilities, and exact national, regional, and chapter assessments can vary. Candidates should rely only on official financial information provided for the specific membership opportunity they are pursuing.
