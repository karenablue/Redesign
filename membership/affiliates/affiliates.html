Ages 8–11

Rhosebud Affiliate

The earliest step in the Sigma Gamma Rho affiliate journey—introducing girls to sisterhood years before they're old enough to join a Rhoer Club.

Sigma Gamma Rho members serve as mentors, guiding each Rhosebud toward becoming a well-rounded young woman equipped to succeed academically and give back to her community. Activities are age-appropriate and hands-on, laying an early foundation in self-confidence, creativity, and civic awareness.

Ages 12–18 • Founded 1939

Rhoer Affiliate

Carrying Founder Mary Lou Allison Gardner Little's original vision for youth development into a new generation of Rhoers.

Rhoer Clubs are sponsored by local alumnae chapters and operate at the local, regional, and national level, numbering more than 500 worldwide. The program blends academic and personal counseling with a rites-of-passage curriculum, preparing members for adulthood with confidence and a lasting commitment to service.

Friends of Sigma • Founded 1980

Philo Affiliate

Professional women who are not college-initiated members but who actively support the Sorority's mission and programs.

Sigma Gamma Rho has worked with non-Greek-affiliated women throughout its history. Beginning in 1943, these supporters were organized into local auxiliary groups under a range of local names, with early roots in the Galveston–Houston area; in 1954 the Sorority formally adopted “Philo”—from the Greek for “friend”—as the affiliate's official designation, and in 1980 it became a national organization under Evelyn H. Hood, the Sorority's 14th International Grand Basileus. It has since grown to more than 1,000 members across the United States and internationally, working alongside the Sorority on service projects, educational and cultural programming, community outreach, and fundraising.
